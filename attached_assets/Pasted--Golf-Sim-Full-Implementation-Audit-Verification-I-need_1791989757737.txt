## Features to Audit

### 1. Mobile Calendar Display
Verify that the public booking calendar is no longer cut off at the bottom on mobile devices.

Check mobile viewport sizing, overflow, modal positioning, scrolling, and calendar responsiveness.

### 2. Automatic Calendar Closure
Verify that selecting a date automatically closes the date picker and updates the available booking times.

### 3. Admin Booking Cutoff Date
Verify that administrators can block online reservations until a selected date, and that the restriction persists and is enforced by the booking backend.

### 4. Full-Day and Partial-Day Closures
Verify that admins can mark full days or specific time ranges as closed.

Check whether customers are prevented from booking these periods, closures can be edited or removed, and existing reservations are protected.

### 5. Recurring Reservations — April 30
Verify that recurring reservations use April 30 as the fixed season-end date, with the correct applicable year.

### 6. Tuesday Unchecked by Default
Verify that Tuesday is unchecked by default when creating a recurring reservation but remains manually selectable.

### 7. Admin Calendar Date Picker
Verify that admins can select a specific date and navigate directly to it.

### 8. Bay 4 Always Red
Verify that Bay 4 is consistently represented in red throughout the relevant admin calendar views, including reservation blocks.

### 9. Reservation Block Height Bug
Verify that reservations visually end at their actual scheduled end time.

Specifically investigate the reported issue where a reservation ending at 4:00 PM visually extends to 4:30 PM.

Check calendar positioning, duration calculations, timezone handling, and grid alignment.

### 10. Drag-and-Drop Between Bays
Verify that admins can move reservations between bays and that:
- The database updates correctly.
- The reservation changes to the destination bay's color.
- Booking conflicts are prevented.
- Invalid moves revert safely.
- Original date, time, and duration are preserved.

### 11. Manual Admin Reservations
Verify that admins can create reservations manually, with appropriate customer information, date, time, duration, and bay selection.

### 12. Duplicate Reservations
Verify that admins can duplicate an existing reservation.

Check that a new reservation ID is generated, the original is preserved, availability is validated, and payments or notifications are not accidentally duplicated.

### 13. Customer Email Database & CSV Export
Verify that:
- Customer emails are collected from bookings.
- The admin dashboard contains an email database.
- Duplicate addresses are handled.
- All customer emails can be exported to CSV without date filtering.
- CSV formatting works correctly.
- Marketing consent is optional, explicit, and not prechecked.
- Consent status is recorded.
- Marketing-eligible exports exclude customers without valid consent.
- The privacy policy describes email collection and marketing use.
- Admin access is protected.

Identify any potential compliance gaps relating to Quebec Law 25 and Canadian anti-spam legislation (CASL). Do not claim legal compliance is guaranteed.

### 14. Existing Statistics in Bottom Admin Dropdown
Verify that the existing statistics have been moved or made accessible through the admin dropdown at the bottom of the page.

The statistics should be hidden from the main view by default.

No existing metrics or calculations should have been removed or unnecessarily recreated.

### 15. Admin Login Password Visibility
Verify that the admin login page contains a working show/hide password toggle, with passwords hidden by default.
