---
name: Booking email policy
description: Approved notification scope and separation of booking success from email delivery.
---

Booking confirmations to the customer and owner apply to website bookings only, not admin-created or recurring bookings.

**Why:** The user explicitly selected “Website bookings only” when asked which reservations should trigger the two branded emails.

**How to apply:** Do not add notifications to staff, league, editing, or Calendar-import flows without approval.

Email delivery failure must not invalidate an already saved reservation.

**Why:** Treating a mail-provider failure as a booking failure can prompt duplicate reservations. Provider acceptance also does not guarantee inbox delivery.

**How to apply:** Keep booking confirmation and email status separate, and do not promise delivery when acceptance cannot be confirmed.
